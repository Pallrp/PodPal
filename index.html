Play History Management

PodPal can track which players have played together to help create more diverse pairings over time. You can export this history to save it, or import a previously saved history file.

How to Use PodPal

Adding and Managing Players
  1. Enter a player name and select one or more power levels
  2. Click "Add Player" to add them to the signup list
  3. Use BL/WL buttons to manage blacklist/whitelist for each player
  4. Click the red X to remove a player
Creating Pods
  1. Click "Cook" to generate an optimized pod arrangement
  2. Click "Shuffle" for a random arrangement
  3. Click "Add Pod" to create an additional empty pod
  4. Click "Remove Pod" to remove the last pod
Managing Pods
  1. Drag and Drop: Move players between pods by dragging them
  2. Player Swapping: Drag a player directly onto another player to swap their positions
  3. Re-evaluate Solution: After manual changes, click to recalculate the solution score
  4. Save Solution: Store the current arrangement for later reference
Tips
  • Players with similar power levels work best together
  • The score indicates the quality of the match - lower is better
  • Use the blacklist to prevent specific players from being paired
  • Use the whitelist to keep friends together
  • Play history helps create diverse pairings over multiple events
